# Labelling Guide

## Labels

| Label | Matlab |
|---|---|
| scam | Paisa, OTP, fake prize, fake link, game-currency ya free-gift ki thagi |
| grooming | Koi bada ya anjaan bachche se secrecy, akele milne, gifts ya bharosa jeetne ki koshish kare |
| bullying | Dhamki, majaak udana, group se alag karna, sharminda karna |
| inappropriate | Gandi ya adult baatein, bina grooming pattern ke |
| safe | Normal baatein, koi khatra nahi |

## Risk levels
- low: halka sa shak, dhyan rakhne layak
- medium: pakka nahi, par parent ko batana chahiye
- high: saaf khatra, turant action chahiye

## Niyam
1. Agar do labels lagte hain, to zyada khatarnak wala do (grooming > bullying > scam > inappropriate > safe).
2. Dost ke beech ka majaak, jisme dhamki ya sharminda karna nahi, wo safe hai.
3. Sirf ek message se pakka pata na chale to risk medium rakho, high nahi.
4. Har example ka reason ek line mein likho.

## Borderline examples (isme aur jodte jao)
- "kal school ke baad milte hain, surprise party hai, kisi ko mat batana" -> safe (dost ke beech surprise, koi bada ya dabaav nahi)
